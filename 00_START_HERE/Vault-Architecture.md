# Vault Architecture

The Vault is organized around four knowledge modes:

- **Knowledge:** concepts, technologies and architecture patterns.
- **Operations:** runbooks, checklists and procedures.
- **Troubleshooting:** symptom-driven diagnosis and recovery.
- **AI:** prompts, workflows, AI-assisted analysis and retrieval.

## Folder layers

```text
01_CORE → foundational concepts
02–14   → technology domains
15      → AI for DevOps
16–18   → troubleshooting and operations
19–20   → reusable templates and prompts
21–22   → architecture patterns and cheat sheets
23      → learning/lab projects
```

## Design rule

Folders provide physical organization. Links and metadata provide semantic organization.

## Product rule

The Vault must remain useful without AI. AI adds acceleration and reasoning support; it is not the only source of value.
