# Product Positioning

## One sentence

AI DevOps Engineer Vault is a structured Obsidian knowledge system that combines DevOps knowledge, troubleshooting, runbooks, checklists, AI workflows, and RAG/MCP-ready contracts.

## Not a promise

It does not promise autonomous production operations, guaranteed incident resolution, or guaranteed financial results.

## Core value

The product reduces the friction between **knowledge retrieval, diagnosis, documentation, and AI-assisted reasoning**.
