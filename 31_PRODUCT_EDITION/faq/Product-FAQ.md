# Product FAQ

## Is this an Obsidian plugin?

No. It is a Vault: Markdown notes, metadata, workflows, examples, and supporting scripts.

## Do I need Claude?

No. Core documentation and operational workflows work independently. Claude is an optional AI layer.

## Does it modify my infrastructure?

The distributed scenarios are offline. The default architecture requires human approval before any change workflow.

## Can I add my own documentation?

Yes. Follow the metadata and naming conventions in [[Metadata-Schema]] and [[Note-Type-Registry]].

## Can I use Git?

Yes. Git is recommended for versioning and review.
