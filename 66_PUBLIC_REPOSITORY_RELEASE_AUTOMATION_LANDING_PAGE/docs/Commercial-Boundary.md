# Community / Commercial Boundary

## Community Edition

The public repository is the reference distribution. Its role is to make the architecture, local runtime, deterministic demo, knowledge base, bounded connectors and development workflow inspectable and reproducible.

## Future commercial editions

Potential commercial boundaries include:

```text
Community
├── local runtime
├── demo / simulator
├── knowledge and skills
├── bounded connectors
└── basic UI

Pro
├── collaboration
├── advanced integrations
├── managed updates
└── team workflows

Enterprise
├── identity / SSO / RBAC
├── enterprise deployment
├── governance / audit controls
├── support / SLA
└── professional services
```

These are product-design directions, not contractual commitments or a final pricing catalog.

Do not remove or obscure safety restrictions in the Community Edition in order to make the demo appear more autonomous than it is.
