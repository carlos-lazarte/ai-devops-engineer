THE WORKFLOW

From signal to verified action

01 Observe

Prometheus, OpenTelemetry and bounded infrastructure connectors provide evidence.

02 Correlate

Temporal and topology-aware signals become an incident candidate.

03 Investigate

RAG, skills and knowledge context structure the investigation.

04 Plan

The planner produces an explicit, reviewable plan.

05 Approve

Policy, identity and human approval remain the authorization boundary.

06 Verify

Results are checked and recorded instead of assuming success.

The Community Edition reference runtime keeps infrastructure connectors read-only and production execution disabled.

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

Designed around operational evidence, not just chat.

Facts stay separate from hypotheses. Investigations preserve evidence and provenance.

Tools stay behind a policy boundary. Credentials are not authorization.

Simulation stays reproducible. The public demo does not require production infrastructure or a model API key.
